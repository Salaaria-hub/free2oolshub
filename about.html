Our Mission

FreeToolsHub was created with a single purpose: to give anyone on the planet free, instant access to powerful image, PDF, and everyday text utilities without ever leaving their browser. We believe that privacy, speed, and zero-cost access should be the default experience for everyday digital tasks. By performing every operation client-side, we eliminate the need for servers, registrations, or hidden fees. Whether you are a student needing to shrink screenshots, a freelancer preparing a portfolio, or someone who needs a quick QR code, our tools are designed to work instantly on any modern device.

What We Offer

Eight independent tools are available from a single, responsive page, covering the most common visual and document workflows:

  • Image Compressor — batch compression to JPEG with adjustable quality.
  • Image Resizer — resize any picture while preserving aspect ratio.
  • Image Converter — switch between JPG, PNG, and WebP formats.
  • Image to PDF — turn a collection of photos into a printable A4 PDF.
  • PDF Merge — combine multiple PDFs into a single document.
  • QR Code Generator — instantly create scannable codes for URLs or text.
  • Word Counter — live statistics for any block of text.
  • Password Generator — cryptographically strong passwords with full customization.

All eight utilities are 100% free, require no sign-up, and run 100% in the browser. Your files never leave your computer, and the interface is deliberately simple so anyone can complete a task in under a minute.

Privacy First

Because the application does not upload files to any backend, we collect no personal documents and store no file logs. The only state used for navigation is the URL hash fragment (such as #compress-image). We aim to keep everyday utilities fast, transparent, and safe to use on sensitive documents.
