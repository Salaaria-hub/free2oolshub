10 Image Sizes You Need for Social Media (2025 Cheat Sheet)

By FreeToolsHub Editorial Team · Published January 15, 2025 · 6 min read

Nothing undermines a great visual faster than an awkward auto-crop that slices off a headline or turns a sharp graphic blurry. Every social platform enforces its own aspect ratios and compression pipelines. Exporting your images at the exact recommended pixel dimensions ensures crisp rendering, avoids unwanted cropping, and keeps your brand looking polished across every feed.

Why Image Size Matters

When you upload an oversized or oddly proportioned image, social networks automatically resize and re-compress it on their servers—often aggressively. Worse, feeds crop tall or wide graphics to fit fixed preview cards, hiding key calls to action. Posts with clean, full-bleed framing consistently earn higher click-through rates and longer dwell time, which feeds directly into platform engagement algorithms.

Instagram Image Sizes

Instagram supports three main feed ratios, plus vertical full-screen formats:

  • Square Post (1080 × 1080 px): Classic 1:1 format that looks balanced in both the feed and your profile grid.
  • Portrait Post (1080 × 1350 px): The 4:5 portrait ratio occupies 25% more vertical screen space on mobile than a square post, making it the highest-performing feed size.
  • Stories, Reels & IGTV Covers (1080 × 1920 px): Full 9:16 vertical canvas. Keep text and logos away from the top 250 px and bottom 250 px so interface buttons do not cover them.

Facebook Image Sizes

  • Feed & Link Preview Image (1200 × 630 px): Ideal for shared articles, promos, and landscape photos without black bars.
  • Page Cover Photo (851 × 315 px minimum, 1640 × 856 px for Retina): Displays differently on desktop versus mobile, so center your primary subject.
  • Facebook Stories (1080 × 1920 px): Shares the same 9:16 vertical dimensions as Instagram Stories.

Twitter/X Image Sizes

For single-image posts on Twitter/X, 1600 × 900 px (16:9) displays cleanly in both desktop and mobile timelines without trimming the top or bottom. For header banners, use 1500 × 500 px (3:1) and leave breathing room near the bottom-left where your profile avatar overlaps.

LinkedIn Image Sizes

LinkedIn link previews and landscape feed graphics perform best at 1200 × 627 px (1.91:1). Company page banners use 1128 × 191 px, while personal profile backgrounds look sharpest at 1584 × 396 px.

YouTube Thumbnails & Banners

  • Video Thumbnail (1280 × 720 px): Must be 16:9, at least 640 px wide, and under 2 MB in JPG, PNG, or WebP.
  • Channel Banner (2560 × 1440 px): YouTube crops the banner differently on TVs, desktops, and phones. Place all essential text inside the central 1546 × 423 px safe zone.

TikTok Video Dimensions

TikTok videos and photo carousel slides are built for 1080 × 1920 px (9:16 vertical). Exporting at 1080 px width prevents TikTok from upscaling low-resolution clips or blurring overlay captions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I upload an image smaller than 1080 px to Instagram?

Instagram upscales any image narrower than 320 px and noticeably softens images under 1080 px wide on high-DPI smartphone screens.

Can I use one image size across all platforms?

A 1200 × 630 px graphic works well across Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter/X, while 1080 × 1920 px works across Instagram Stories, Facebook Stories, and TikTok.

Should I compress social media images before uploading?

Light compression (80–85% quality) keeps uploads fast and within platform limits like YouTube's 2 MB thumbnail cap. Try our Image Compressor.

Why do my PNG logos look blurry on Facebook?

Uploading a tiny logo under 500 px wide forces Facebook to upscale it. Export logos at least 1000 px wide before uploading.
